# Foundation: the quality floor every family must meet

This page holds rules only. Colours, fonts and layouts belong to each family. A family can raise these bars but never lower them.

1. **One job per slide.** Each slide answers one question or makes one point, stated in its title.
2. **Readable from the back of the room.** Body text is at least 24 pt and labels at least 18 pt on the 1440×810 pt canvas.
   If content doesn't fit, split it across states or slides. Never shrink it.
3. **Contrast.** Text is at least 4.5:1 against its background (3:1 at 36 pt and above). Colour is never the only cue where it matters.
4. **Real titles.** Every content slide puts its title in the title placeholder, so the PDF outline, navigation and
   accessibility work.
5. **Figures are legible and credited.** At least 150 effective ppi on the canvas. No pasted masks or debris.
   Every figure you didn't make carries a short source credit.
6. **Answers are computed and checked**, never transcribed.
7. **Progression.** A lecture shows where it is going (a map), where it is (sections), and what was learned (a summary).
   Derivations and worked solutions are revealed step by step in the presenting deck. The student PDF shows each slide's final state.
8. **Consistency.** The same kind of content looks the same everywhere in a module: titles, examples, results, equations.
9. **Decoration only on structural slides** (title, section, closing). Content slides carry content.
10. **Light by default.** Dark text on a light background, unless a module decision says otherwise.
